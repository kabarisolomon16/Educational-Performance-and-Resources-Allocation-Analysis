# Educational Performance and Resources Allocation Analyst Dashboard

An interactive educational analytics dashboard designed to monitor academic performance, evaluate student welfare, and analyze the allocation and utilization of educational resources. This project transforms educational data into actionable insights that support evidence based decision-making, institutional performance monitoring and effective resource management.

The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of academic excellence, student welfare, institutional performance and resource distribution, helping school administrators, educators and other stakeholders identify performance gaps and make informed strategic decisions.

## Project Overview

The **Educational Performance and Resources Allocation Analyst Dashboard** consists of two interconnected analytical dashboards:

1. **Academic Excellence Dashboard**
2. **Student Welfare & Resources Dashboard**

Together, these dashboards provide insights into students' academic achievements, attendance, financial status, learning support, counselling services, and educational resource utilization.

## Project Objectives

The primary objectives of this project are to:

1. Monitor and evaluate students' academic performance across different grades, departments, and academic terms.
2. Identify students who may require additional academic intervention and welfare support.
3. Analyze the relationship between study hours, attendance, and academic achievement.
4. Evaluate the distribution and utilization of educational resources.
5. Monitor students' financial accessibility through school fee payment analysis.
6. Assess access to essential learning resources, including internet connectivity and personal devices.
7. Evaluate the effectiveness of student support programmes, including lunch programmes, counselling, and learning support.
8. Provide actionable insights to support educational planning, resource allocation, and institutional improvement.


##  Key Analytical Areas

1. Analytical Area and Description 
2. Academic Performance: Evaluates average scores, grades and examination outcomes.
3. Departmental Analysis: Compares academic performance across departments.
4. Student Welfare: Identifies students requiring additional assistance.
5. Attendance Analysis: Examines attendance patterns and their relationship with academic outcomes.
6. Financial Accessibility: Monitors fee payment status and outstanding balances.
7. Learning Resources: Evaluates access to internet connectivity and personal devices.
8. Support Programmes: Assesses participation in lunch, counselling, and learning support programmes.
9. Resource Allocation : Identifies resource gaps and opportunities for improved distribution.


##  Interactive Dashboard Capabilities

The dashboards provide interactive filtering options that allow users to explore educational data from different perspectives.

Available filters include:

- Department
- Academic Year
- Academic Term
- Grade
- Age

These filters enable users to investigate specific student groups, compare departmental outcomes, and identify areas requiring targeted interventions.

## Key Insights and Potential Applications

The dashboard supports educational stakeholders in answering important questions, such as:

- Which departments demonstrate stronger academic performance?
- Which grades require additional academic intervention?
- Is there a relationship between study hours and students' average scores?
- How does student attendance relate to academic achievement?
- What proportion of students have outstanding school fees?
- How many students lack reliable internet access or personal devices?
- Which students require learning support or counselling?
- Are existing welfare programmes reaching the students who need them?
- Where should additional educational resources be allocated?

These insights can support evidence-based planning, improve institutional accountability, and promote better educational outcomes.
